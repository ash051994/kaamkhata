KaamKhata privacy policy

Last updated: 11 October 2026

KaamKhata is a register app for labour contractors. It is built so that your data stays on your own phone.

What the app stores

Details you enter about your firm, the companies you supply workers to, and your workers: names, contact numbers, addresses, ID numbers (Aadhaar, PAN, UAN, ESIC), bank details, photos of documents, attendance, wages and payments.

Where it is stored

Only on your device, inside the app's private storage. KaamKhata has no server and no account. The developer cannot see, collect or receive any of this data. Nothing is sold or shared.

Permissions

  • Microphone — only when you tap the microphone to fill a worker's details by voice. Speech is turned into text by your phone's built-in speech service (for example Google speech services), under that provider's own privacy terms. KaamKhata does not record or keep audio.
  • Camera / photos — only when you choose to take or pick a photo of a worker or a document.

Backups and reports

When you export a backup file or a report (PDF or CSV), the file is created on your device and you choose where to send it, such as WhatsApp, email or Google Drive. After that, the file is handled by the app or service you chose.

Deleting your data

Use More → Backup and import → Erase everything, or uninstall the app. Both remove all data from the device.

Workers' information

As the contractor, you are responsible for collecting your workers' personal information with their knowledge and for keeping it safe. You can turn on a PIN lock in Settings.

Children

The app is meant for business use by adults and is not directed at children.
